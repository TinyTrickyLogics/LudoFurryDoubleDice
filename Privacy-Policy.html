1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features enabled in the game, we may process information such as:

  • Profile & Multiplayer Information: User profile picture and display name provided by you or captured via device camera/gallery to be used in multiplayer gameplay matches.
  • Device and technical information: device model, operating system/version, language, app version, approximate diagnostic information, IP address, and other technical identifiers.
  • Game and usage information: gameplay events, settings, progress, session information, performance information, crash information, and multiplayer interactions.
  • Purchase information: when you make in-app purchases (such as coins and gems), transactions are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We receive non-financial transaction details like product identifiers and purchase status.
  • Push Notifications Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to deliver game-related notifications and updates.
  • Information you send to us: support requests, feedback, or direct communications.
Important: Data collection strictly corresponds to active features, SDKs, and permissions implemented in the live application[cite: 1].
📱

2. Device Permissions Usage

Our app requests specific device permissions to deliver core multiplayer and interactive features:

  • Camera Permission: Used solely when you choose to capture or update your custom multiplayer profile picture from within the game. Images are processed locally and for multiplayer profile display.
  • Microphone Permission: Used for in-game voice chat features during live multiplayer matches to communicate with other players. Audio is transmitted in real-time during matches and is not permanently recorded or stored by us.
  • Notification Permission (FCM): Used via Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to send you alerts regarding game turns, rewards, events, and important updates.
User Control: You can grant or revoke camera, microphone, and notification permissions at any time through your Android device settings.
⚙️

3. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • provide and operate multiplayer matches, displaying user profile pictures and names to other players;
  • manage voice chat and camera features during gameplay;
  • process in-app purchases for virtual items like coins and gems;
  • deliver push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM);
  • save and synchronize game progress, settings, and stats;
  • detect crashes, fix bugs, and optimize performance;
  • ensure game security, prevent cheating, fraud, and abuse;
  • comply with legal obligations and Google Play requirements.
